6. Presente simple (Present simple)

Se usa para hábitos y rutinas. Con he, she, it se agrega -s al verbo.

  • I play soccer. → She plays soccer.
  • We study English. → He studies English.
  • Negativo: I don't like coffee. / He doesn't like coffee.
  • Pregunta: Do you work? / Does she work?
